Your whole money life, the way it works in Bangladesh

Most money apps stop at what you spent today. Taka follows your savings, loans and insurance too, and counts what you owe, not only what you have.

Made for Bangladesh

bKash, Nagad, Rocket, every major bank and the finance companies, with their logos. Amounts in lakh and crore (৳3,97,296), with Sanchayapatra rates and tax built in.

Know what you can spend

Home shows the cash, wallet and bank money you can use today, with your net worth below. A daily limit follows this month's income and updates every day.

Know where your money is safe

A warning before a deposit goes above the Tk 2 lakh that is protected if a bank closes, or when the bank has been merged.

Tells you what's next

EMIs, premiums and DPS instalments due this week sit on Home, with a reminder a day before. Savings show months left and the maturity value.

Private by design

No sign-up and no server. Your data stays on your phone in an encrypted database, behind an optional PIN or fingerprint. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

Bangla first

The whole app works in Bangla or English, with Bangla or English digits. The Bangla reads the way banks and bKash talk, not like a translation.

Install in a minute

Taka isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the APK file straight from here.

  1. Download

    On your Android phone, tap Download for Android to get the .apk file.

  2. Open it

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.

  3. Set up or restore

    Pick Bangla or English and follow the short setup, or tap Restore a backup to bring your data from another phone.

Moving from FinUtopia? Installing Taka over it keeps all your data. Moving from Taka Tracker? Export an encrypted backup there (Settings, Backup), then choose Restore a backup on Taka's first screen.

Your money stays yours

No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. Google Drive backup is optional and encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves the phone. The only network calls are that backup and the daily update check.

Good to know

  • Interest, tax and EMI figures are careful estimates. Your bank, bKash or post office statement is the final word, and every figure can be edited.
  • Taka is an independent app. It is not affiliated with bKash, City Bank or any bank, wallet or insurer shown. Their names and logos belong to their owners and are shown only to help you recognise your accounts.
  • Taka never moves money and never asks for a bank or wallet PIN, OTP or password.
